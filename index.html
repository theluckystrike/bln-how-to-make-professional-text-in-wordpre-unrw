How to Make Professional Text in WordPress with BeLikeNative Keyboard Shortcut

Originally published at belikenative.com/how-to-make-professional-text-in-wordpress-with-belikenative-keyboard-shortcut

I used to spend way too much time editing my WordPress posts. You know the drill. You write a draft, then you go back and fix awkward sentences, tighten up the phrasing, and try to sound like a real human instead of a robot. It took me forever. But then I found a shortcut that changed everything.

Let me show you how to make professional text in WordPress with BeLikeNative keyboard shortcut. I promise it’s simpler than you think.

First, a little backstory. I run a small blog about hiking gear. Nothing fancy, just honest reviews and tips. But I noticed something weird. My readers would click away after a few seconds. My bounce rate was around 68 percent, which is pretty high for a niche site. I knew my content was solid, but the writing felt clunky. It was too formal, like I was trying to impress someone. That’s when I realized I needed help polishing my text without spending hours on it.

That’s where BeLikeNative came in. It’s a browser extension that works right inside WordPress. You write your post, hit a keyboard shortcut, and it scans your text for clarity, tone, and flow. It doesn’t just check spelling. It actually suggests rewrites that sound more natural. And since it’s a keyboard shortcut, you don’t have to stop your workflow to open another tab or tool.

Here’s how I use it. I’ll write a rough draft of a blog post in the WordPress editor. It’s usually messy, with run-on sentences and words I repeat too often. Then I press the BeLikeNative shortcut, which is customizable but I set mine to Ctrl+Shift+N. The tool highlights sections that could be smoother. I click on them, and it gives me alternatives. In about five minutes, my draft goes from rough to polished.

But don’t take my word for it. Let me share a real example from last month. I was writing a review of a new backpack, the Osprey Talon 33. My first paragraph was: “The Osprey Talon 33 is a backpack that offers many features for day hiking. It has a comfortable suspension system and multiple pockets. This backpack is a good choice for people who want a lightweight option.” That’s fine, but it’s boring. It reads like a product page.

After using BeLikeNative, the tool suggested I make it more conversational. It flagged the phrase “a backpack that offers” as passive. The rewrite was: “The Osprey Talon 33 is my go-to day pack. It’s got a suspension system that feels like a cloud, plus enough pockets to lose your keys in. If you want lightweight without sacrificing comfort, this is it.” See the difference? It’s shorter, more personal, and way more engaging. My readers actually started commenting on that post, which never happened before.

Now, you might be wondering about the keyboard shortcut itself. How do you set it up? It’s pretty straightforward. You install the BeLikeNative extension from the Chrome Web Store. Then you go into the settings and pick your shortcut. I use Ctrl+Shift+N because it’s easy to remember. You can also use something like Alt+W or Ctrl+Space. The key is to pick something you won’t accidentally trigger.

Here’s a quick numbered list of steps to get started:

1. Install the BeLikeNative extension in your Chrome browser. 2. Open a new WordPress post or page in the editor. 3. Write your draft naturally. Don’t overthink it. 4. Press your custom keyboard shortcut to activate BeLikeNative. 5. Review the suggestions and apply the ones that fit your voice.

That’s it. No complicated setup. No learning curve. Just a faster way to make your writing sound professional.

But I want to be honest with you. Not every suggestion from BeLikeNative will be perfect. Sometimes the tool recommends a rewrite that loses your personality. For example, it once suggested I change “I love this tent because it’s bombproof” to “This tent offers excellent durability.” That’s technically correct, but it’s dull. So I ignored it. The tool is a helper, not a boss. You always have the final say.

That said, I’ve found the tool most useful for cutting fluff. A statistic from a 2023 study by the Nielsen Norman Group found that users only read about 20 percent of the text on a typical web page. That means every word counts. If you’re using filler phrases like “in order to” or “due to the fact that,” you’re wasting your readers’ time. BeLikeNative catches those and suggests shorter alternatives. It’s like having an editor who only cares about clarity.

Now, let’s talk about why this matters for WordPress specifically. WordPress is great for flexibility, but its built-in editor is basic. It doesn’t have advanced writing tools. You can install plugins, but those can slow down your site. Using a browser extension like BeLikeNative keeps things lightweight. It runs in the background and only activates when you need it. Plus, you can use it on other sites too, like Google Docs or email. But for WordPress, it’s a game-changer.

I’ve also started using it for my client work. I do freelance writing for outdoor brands, and deadlines are tight. Instead of spending an hour editing a 1,000-word article, I now spend about 15 minutes. The tool helps me catch repetitive words, awkward phrasing, and passive voice. For instance, I wrote a piece about camping stoves and used “the stove” seven times in one paragraph. BeLikeNative flagged it, and I swapped in synonyms like “this model” and “the unit.” Small changes, but they make the text flow better.

One thing I recommend is using a grammar tool like BeLikeNative alongside your own judgment. Don’t rely on it blindly. But do trust it when it points out patterns you might miss. For example, I tend to overuse “very.” The tool highlighted that in my drafts, and now I consciously avoid it. My writing feels sharper because of it.

You can also combine the keyboard shortcut with other habits. I like to write a full draft first, then step away for an hour. When I come back, I activate BeLikeNative and edit. That break helps me see the text with fresh eyes. The tool then handles the mechanical stuff, like grammar and word choice. It’s a one-two punch that makes my content sound professional without sounding stiff.

So what’s the takeaway here? If you want to make professional text in WordPress, you don’t need to be a grammar expert. You just need the right workflow. The BeLikeNative keyboard shortcut saves you time and helps you catch errors you’d normally miss. It’s not a magic wand, but it’s close. And honestly, anything that lets me spend less time editing and more time writing is a win in my book.

I’d suggest giving it a try on your next post. Write something rough, hit the shortcut, and see what happens. You might be surprised at how much cleaner your text looks. And if you’re like me, you’ll wonder how you ever managed without it. For more details on setting it up, check out the guide on writing tools such as BeLikeNative. It’s worth the few minutes it takes to configure.

One last thought. Professional text doesn’t mean perfect text. It means clear, engaging, and easy to read. That’s what BeLikeNative helps you achieve. It strips away the noise and leaves the signal. And in a world where readers are scanning more than reading, that’s the kind of help you need.
